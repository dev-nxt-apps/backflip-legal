# Muscle Recovery Tracking

Training the same muscle before it has recovered is how progress stalls.
**Backflip** tracks recovery for **21 muscle groups** and shows it on a 3D
body you can turn and tap.

## Recovery that learns you

A flat "wait 72 hours" rule is wrong for almost everyone. Backflip starts from
per-muscle recovery windows and adjusts them to how *you* actually recover,
using your training history, how hard you rated your sessions, and — if you
connect Apple Health — your sleep and heart-rate variability.

## The map is a control, not a picture

- **See it.** Worked, recovering, ready, or not trained yet — at a glance.
- **Use it.** Tap a muscle to filter the exercise library to movements that
  train it.
- **It decides your session.** The [coach](/backflip-legal/ai-workout-coach)
  builds around what is recovered, so you are not squatting on legs that are
  still repairing.

## Warm-ups and cool-downs that match the day

Each session can open with mobility work for the muscles you are about to
train and close with a cool-down for the ones you did — matched to the
session, not a generic list.

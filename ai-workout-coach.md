# AI Workout Coach for Weight Lifting

Most apps hand you a fixed program. **Backflip** builds today's session from
what your body and your week actually look like — and tells you why.

## What the coach reads

- **Recovery, per muscle group.** Not a flat 72-hour rule — each group has its
  own clock, learned from how you train. [More on recovery](/backflip-legal/muscle-recovery)
- **Your rhythm.** Which days you actually train, and what you have covered
  over the last seven days.
- **Readiness.** Sleep and heart-rate variability from Apple Health when you
  connect it, plus how hard you rated recent sessions.
- **Your equipment and where you are.** Gym, home, hotel — the plan only ever
  contains movements you can actually perform.

## It adapts mid-workout, not just before

If a working set comes up short, the coach offers the next one lighter and
says why: *"That set came up 3 reps short. Want the next one at 90 kg?"* You
decide — it suggests, it never rewrites your session behind your back.

## Every decision is explained

When the coach goes easier, it names the reason rather than silently shrinking
your session. A plan you do not understand is a plan you stop trusting.

## Eight-week blocks with a finish line

Running has races. Lifting rarely has a date to point at, so Backflip supplies
one: eight-week blocks that end in a test week and a PR attempt.

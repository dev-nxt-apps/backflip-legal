# Backflip — Support

**Backflip** is a free, offline-first fitness app for iPhone and Apple Watch:
log workouts, follow generated plans, and track your progress — with your
data stored on your device.

## Get help

Questions, bug reports, feature requests, or privacy requests:

- **Email:** [backflip.customersupport@gmail.com](mailto:backflip.customersupport@gmail.com)

We aim to respond within a few days (privacy requests within 30 days).

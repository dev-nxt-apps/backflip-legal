# Privacy Policy for Backflip

**Effective Date:** July 22, 2026
**Last Updated:** August 17, 2026

SimplerApps ("we," "our," or "us") built Backflip as a free fitness app. This
Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when
you use Backflip.

By using Backflip, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

This policy applies to **all users regardless of region**. We apply a single,
privacy-protective standard that meets the requirements of India's DPDP Act, the
EU/UK GDPR and ePrivacy rules, and the California CCPA.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly

- **Account information:** You sign in with Apple to use Backflip. We receive
  your Apple ID and the email address you choose to share (which may be an
  Apple private-relay address).
- **Fitness profile:** Information you enter during onboarding and in your
  profile — such as training goals, experience level, training days, preferred
  units, and any injuries or areas you ask us to train around.
- **Workout data:** The workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weights, rest periods,
  routines, schedules, and body-weight entries you log.
- **Support requests:** If you contact us, we collect your email address and the
  contents of your message.

### Early-Access Waitlist (Optional, OFF by default)

Backflip sometimes offers a waiting list for a feature we have not released yet
— today, the **Guided Library**. It is entirely optional, and nothing is
collected unless you ask to be on it.

- **What we collect:** the email address **you type**. We never pre-fill it from
  your account, so if you type nothing, we collect nothing. We also record which
  price option you were shown and the date you joined. No workout data, no
  health data, and nothing else.
- **Why we collect it:** so we can send you **one** message — when that feature
  launches — and so we can see how many people wanted it at each price. It is
  used for no other message and no other purpose.
- **Lawful basis:** your **consent**, given by ticking the "Email me when Guided
  Library launches" box. That box is unticked by default, and you cannot join
  without ticking it.
- **Sharing:** we do **not** sell, rent, or trade this address. We do not add it
  to any other mailing list, and we do not use it to advertise anything else.
- **Retention:** until the feature launches and that one announcement is sent,
  or until you withdraw — whichever comes first — and in no case longer than 24
  months from the day you joined.
- **How to withdraw:** open the Guided Library card in the app and tap
  **Leave the list**. That erases your address from your device. If we had
  already received it, email backflip.customersupport@gmail.com and we will erase our copy
  too; we respond within 30 days.

As of this version the waitlist endpoint is not configured, so no address leaves
your device at all: a join is held on your device only, and leaving the list
erases it outright.

### Health & Fitness Data (Apple Health)

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Backflip reads only the specific data
types you authorize, to power your Insights and readiness features:

- **Active energy burned**
- **Heart rate**, **resting heart rate**, and **heart rate variability (SDNN)**
- **Sleep analysis**
- **Step count**
- **Body mass (weight)**
- **Workouts** saved by your Apple Watch

If you tap **Import from Apple Health** in Personal info, Backflip additionally
reads **biological sex**, **date of birth**, **height** and **body mass** — once,
to prefill your profile.

Backflip writes to Apple Health only when you complete a workout on your phone
**without** a paired Apple Watch, so the session still reaches the Fitness app.
It writes **workouts**, their **active energy burned**, their **distance** (for
walks, runs, hikes, rides, rows, and skating sessions), and the **workout effort
score** you rate.

- We access Health data **only with your explicit permission**, granted through
  Apple's own Health permission screens, and only the specific data types you
  approve.
- Health data read from Apple Health stays **on your device**. We do **not** use
  Health data for advertising or marketing, we do **not** sell it, and we do
  **not** share it with third parties or use it for any purpose other than
  providing the features you requested.
- You can revoke Backflip's Health access at any time in the Apple Health app.

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Anonymous usage analytics (optional, OFF by default):** If — and only if —
  you turn on "Share anonymous usage analytics" in Settings, Backflip collects
  anonymous, aggregate feature-usage events (for example, which screens are
  used). These events are **not linked to your identity** and **never include
  the contents of your workouts or your health data**. This is disabled by
  default; nothing is collected unless you opt in, and turning it off deletes
  anything queued for upload. As of this version the analytics endpoint is not
  configured, so no usage data leaves the device.

### Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect precise location.
- We do not track you across other apps or websites.
- We do not build advertising profiles.
- We do not sell your data.

## 2. How We Store Your Information

Backflip is **offline-first**. Your account, profile, and workout data are stored
**locally on your device** in an on-device database. We do not operate a cloud
account server for this data, and your data is not transmitted to us except, if
you opt in, the anonymous analytics and the early-access waitlist address
described in Section 1.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide and maintain Backflip's core functionality (logging, plan generation,
  Insights, readiness);
- Generate rules-based workout suggestions from your own data (see our
  [generator provenance note](https://dev-nxt-apps.github.io/backflip-legal/generator-provenance));
- Fix bugs and resolve technical issues;
- Respond to your support requests;
- Tell you once, at launch, about a feature you asked to be notified about (only
  if you join the early-access waitlist);
- Understand aggregate usage patterns to guide product development (only if you
  opt in to analytics).

We do **not** use your information to sell your data, build advertising
profiles, or make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly
significant effects about you.

---

## 4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Because your data is
stored locally on your device, there is normally no sharing at all. In limited
circumstances we may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or
valid legal process, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of
SimplerApps, our users, or the public.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Account, profile, and workout data:** Retained on your device for as long
  as you keep your account. Deleted when you delete your account or the app.
- **Anonymous analytics (if enabled):** Aggregated and anonymized; not linked to
  you. Turning analytics off deletes anything queued on your device.
- **Early-access waitlist address (if you joined):** Until the announcement is
  sent or you leave the list, whichever comes first, and never more than 24
  months. Leaving the list erases the copy held on your device immediately.
- **Health data:** Not retained by us — it is read from Apple Health on demand
  and stays on your device.

## 7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your
information, including:

- HTTPS/TLS for any network communication;
- Local, on-device storage of your personal data by default.

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take commercially
reasonable steps to protect your data.

## 8. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information, which you can
exercise directly in the app:

- **Access & Export:** Settings → Your data & privacy → **Export my data**
  produces a portable copy of your personal data.
- **Correction:** Edit your profile, goals, and workout history at any time in
  the app.
- **Deletion:** Settings → **Delete Account** permanently removes your profile,
  goals, and workout history from your device.
- **Withdraw consent:** Turn off analytics at any time in Settings; leave the
  early-access waitlist from the Guided Library card in the app; revoke Health
  access in the Apple Health app. Withdrawing is always at least as easy as
  giving consent was.

For any request we cannot fulfill in-app, contact us at backflip.customersupport@gmail.com. We
will respond within 30 days.

### For European Union / United Kingdom Residents (GDPR)

- **Data controller:** SimplerApps, backflip.customersupport@gmail.com
- **Lawful basis:** consent (analytics, Health access, early-access waitlist),
  performance of a contract (providing the service you requested), and our
  legitimate interests (improving and securing the app).
- **Additional rights:** restriction of processing, objection to processing, and
  the right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority.

### For California Residents (CCPA)

- **Right to know, delete, and correct** your personal information.
- **We do not sell or share** your personal information. If that ever changes,
  we will provide a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" option.
- **Non-discrimination:** we will not discriminate against you for exercising
  your rights.

### For Indian Residents (DPDP)

- **Data fiduciary:** SimplerApps, backflip.customersupport@gmail.com
- **Consent:** we obtain your consent before processing personal data, except
  where permitted by law.
- **Data principal rights:** access, correction, erasure, nominating a
  representative, and filing a grievance with us or the Data Protection Board of
  India.

personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected
such information, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has
provided us with personal information, contact us at backflip.customersupport@gmail.com.

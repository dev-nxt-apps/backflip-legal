# Medical & AI Disclaimer

Backflip's workout plans, readiness scores, and insights are **AI-generated** —
produced automatically by a rules-based system reading your own logged data, not
reviewed by a human trainer or clinician.

**Backflip's plans are AI-generated and are not a substitute for professional
medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before starting any exercise
program.**

Physical exercise carries an inherent risk of injury. You exercise at your own
risk. Stop and seek medical attention if you experience pain, dizziness,
shortness of breath, or any other concerning symptom. See the full Health &
Safety disclaimer in the [Terms of Use](/backflip-legal/terms).

# Log Your Workout by Voice

Your hands are chalky, your phone is across the bench, and you have ninety
seconds. **Say it instead.**

> "Eighty kilos for eight."

Backflip parses that into the right exercise, weight and reps, shows you what
it heard, and logs it when you confirm.

## Built for a gym, not a demo

- **It shows you what it heard** before anything is saved — a number you did
  not say never lands in your history.
- **It handles how people actually talk.** "Bench, 2 plates a side, 8 reps."
  "Same as last time."
- **It picks the right movement** from a catalogue of over 1,300, and asks
  when a name is genuinely ambiguous.
- **Nothing leaves your phone** because of a voice entry — your log is stored
  on your device.

## When you would rather tap

Wheels for every number, no keyboard, with your previous session shown beside
each set. Voice is an option, never the only way in.

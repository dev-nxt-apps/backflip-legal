# Workout Tracker & Gym Log for iPhone

**Backflip** is a workout tracker with a coach built in. Log every set, watch
your lifts climb, and let the app decide what today should be — free, no ads,
and your data stays on your device.

## Logging that keeps up with you

- **Wheel or voice.** Say "eighty kilos for eight" mid-set, or spin the wheels.
  No keyboard between sets.
- **Your last session, right there.** Each set shows what you did last time —
  tap it to match, edit to beat it.
- **One tap ends a set**, starts the rest timer, and updates your volume.
- **Rest per exercise.** A heavy squat can rest three minutes while a curl
  rests forty-five seconds.
- **Plates on the bar.** Optional: see exactly what to load per side.

## Progress you can actually read

Personal records per lift and per rep target, weekly volume by muscle group,
training rhythm, streaks measured in **weeks** — because a rest day is part of
the plan, not a failure — and body metrics that sync with Apple Health.

## What makes it different

Most trackers record what you did. Backflip also decides what to do next: it
models each muscle group's recovery and builds a session around what you are
actually ready for. See [the AI workout coach](/backflip-legal/ai-workout-coach)
and [muscle recovery tracking](/backflip-legal/muscle-recovery).
